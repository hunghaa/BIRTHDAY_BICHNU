## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an artsy, dream-pop celebratory experience designed for milestone personal events and joyful intimate commemorations. It juxtaposes the expressive, sculptural, avant-garde geometry of display typography with pillowy, hyper-rounded pastel interfaces.

### Personality & Tone
- **Dreamy & Luminous:** Soft cloud-like surfaces, ambient chromatic glows, and delicate spatial breathing room create a warm, celestial aura.
- **Sculptural & Playful:** High-fashion, avant-garde letterforms bring artistic reverence to the celebratory subject, elevating personal joy into an editorial experience.
- **Tactile & Tender:** Gentle, pillowy button volumes and translucent card finishes invite physical engagement without visual harshness.

### Visual Style
A deliberate fusion of **Tactile Glassmorphism** and **Avant-Garde Pop**. Interfaces feel weightless yet physically soft—utilizing ultra-diffused roseate drop shadows, frosted milky-pink backdrops, and generous circular contours that emulate buoyant clouds and pastel confections.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-energy chromatic magenta in comforting dairy pastels, confectionary blush, and soft cocoa neutrals.

### Roles & Tokens
- **Primary (`#DB2777`):** Vivid strawberry magenta. Used selectively for high-emphasis interactive states, active indicators, and hero text focal points.
- **Secondary (`#F472B6`):** Warm rose petal. Fuels accent highlights, soft gradient stops, and delicate decorative elements.
- **Tertiary (`#FBCFE8`):** Frosted blush tone. Serves as container borders, secondary pill backgrounds, and soft ambient halo color stops.
- **Background (`#FFFDF9`):** Warm whipped ivory. Replaces stark optical white with a cozy, sunlit glow across page canvases.
- **Surface (`#FFF0F5`):** Sheer lavender-blush. Base fill for cards, modal sheets, and floating containers.
- **Neutral / Text (`#4A3E3D`):** Warm roasted mocha. Replaces cold black or gray to preserve soft, gentle contrast against light pastel surfaces while passing strict WCAG AA contrast ratios.

## Typography

The typography couples dramatic structural presence with round, readable tenderness.

- **Syne (Headlines & Display):** Selected for its artistic, sculptured geometry. Use weight 800 for monumental initials, names, and milestone numbers, and weight 700 for celebratory titles. Syne supplies the high-fashion, dream-pop edge that anchors the soft background.
- **Nunito Sans (Body, Microcopy, & Labels):** Provides rounded terminal terminals and generous aperture, maintaining an affectionate, friendly tone without sacrificing optical clarity at compact scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a floating, centered fluid-column canvas with generous external breathing margins to enhance the feeling of suspended cloud-like modules.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Cards and photo blocks collapse into single stacked columns with rounded tactile tabs.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column fluid grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` outer margins.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of `72rem` (1152px), centered on viewport with `2rem` (32px) gutters.

### Spacing Philosophy
Rhythm alternates between tight, pill-shaped internal padding (`space-xs` to `space-md`) and sweeping vertical section breathability (`space-3xl` and `space-4xl`). This prevents dense clutter and preserves the light, airy celebration aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **multi-layered translucent diffusion** rather than sharp mechanical shadows. Surfaces mimic frosted marshmallow confectionery and glowing ambient bubbles.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#FFFDF9`. No elevation.
- **Level 1 (Pillowy Surfaces & Cards):** Background `rgba(255, 240, 245, 0.75)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border is a subtle translucent stroke (`1.5px solid rgba(251, 207, 232, 0.6)`). Casts an ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.12), 0 2px 8px rgba(74, 62, 61, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Elements & Floating Controls):** Background `rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.95)` with dual soft shadow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.18), 0 4px 12px rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.1)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Lightboxes & Toasts):** Background `#FFFDF9` wrapped in a glowing ambient aura: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.25), 0 0 0 1px rgba(251, 207, 232, 0.8)`.

### Ambient Halos
Decorative focal points (such as portrait frames and celebratory wishes) use background radial glows: `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(251, 207, 232, 0.5) 0%, rgba(255, 253, 249, 0) 70%)` positioned off-center behind surfaces to simulate soft ambient stage lighting.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a hyper-rounded, pill-shaped token model (`roundedness: 3`). Corners are deliberately bulbous, friendly, and smooth, eliminating rigid geometric edges across every component.

### Radius Scale Implementation
- **Small Elements (Pill buttons, badges, chips, tags):** `9999px` (Full continuous pill).
- **Medium Elements (Inputs, photo thumbnails, message cards):** `1.5rem` to `2rem`.
- **Large Surfaces (Hero containers, guestbook panels, modal frames):** `3rem` (48px).
- **Decorative Frames:** Use gentle scalloped or asymmetrical organic radius pairings (e.g., `3rem 3rem 1.5rem 3rem`) to evoke playful confectionery silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill (`border-radius: 9999px`), gradient background from `#DB2777` to `#F472B6`, text `#FFFDF9`, typography `label-lg`. Elevated with a soft strawberry glow. Hover states elevate slightly (`translate-y: -2px`) and expand blur radius.
- **Secondary / Soft Pill:** Translucent `#FFF0F5` fill with `1.5px solid #FBCFE8`, text `#DB2777`. On hover, background deepens to `#FDE2E8`.
- **Icon / Floating Action Button:** Perfectly circular (`w-12 h-12`), frosted milky surface with soft pink ambient drop shadow.

### Cards & Wish Notes
- Floating pillowy containers with `3rem` outer border radius, `1.5px` border in `#FBCFE8`, and `space-xl` internal padding.
- Text content is set in `#4A3E3D`, with decorative dates or signatures formatted in `Syne` bold caps (`label-sm`) in `#DB2777`.

### Chips & Badges
- Miniature full-pill containers with `space-xs` vertical and `space-md` horizontal padding.
- Background: Soft milk blush (`#FDE2E8`), text `#DB2777` with `label-md` tracking.

### Input Fields & Guestbook Text Areas
- Enclosed in `rounded-2xl` boundaries, background `#FFFDF9` with a subtle recessed inner shadow and `1.5px solid #FBCFE8`.
- Focused state transforms the border to `#DB2777` and adds a glowing focus ring: `0 0 0 4px rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.25)`.
- Placeholder text in soft mocha tint `rgba(74, 62, 61, 0.45)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Radios are concentric circular pills; checkboxes feature full smooth `0.625rem` radius corners.
- Selected state fills with `#DB2777`, holding a pristine `#FFFDF9` icon mark.

### Memory & Photo Keepsake Frames
- Double-bordered framing: An outer `3rem` rounded boundary in `#FBCFE8` holding a `space-xs` gap before the inner photograph radius, delivering a collectible sticker or keepsake vignette feel.
